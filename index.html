How the price is worked out

Effective price = listed fare − discounts the site confirms for that flight − card rewards you'd earn. Prices are per adult.

Confirmed discounts include MakeMyTrip flat-₹ codes, EaseMyTrip's extra instant discount, Flipkart's "Savings of ₹X" and Amazon's "₹ off". "Up to" and percentage offers can't be priced until checkout, so they're listed in each cell's details instead.

Tap any cell for the next cheapest flights, every offer the site showed, and a link to the same search on that site.
