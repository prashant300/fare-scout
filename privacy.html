Fare Scout privacy policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Fare Scout is a personal tool made of a web page (prashant300.github.io/fare-scout) and a Chrome extension. The extension compares flight fares across HDFC SmartBuy, Axis Travel EDGE, MakeMyTrip, Yatra, EaseMyTrip, Flipkart and Amazon when you start a scan from the Fare Scout page.

What the extension does

When you press Scan on the Fare Scout page, the extension opens those sites' flight search pages in a separate browser window, reads the flight results shown (airline, flight number, times, stops, fares, fees and offers), and passes them back to the Fare Scout page in the same browser. It does this only for the route and dates you enter.

What data is collected

The extension reads the content of the flight search result pages listed above, only during a scan you start. It does not read any other websites, your browsing history, passwords, payment details or personal information. It never fills in forms, makes bookings or makes payments.

Where data goes

The extension has no server. It does not send any data to the developer or to any third party. Scan results go only to the Fare Scout page open in your own browser. The extension stores one item in Chrome's local extension storage: the address of the Fare Scout page, so its toolbar button can open it.

If you choose to turn on sync in the Fare Scout page, the page (not the extension) saves your scans and settings to a gist in your own GitHub account, using a token you create and that stays in your browser. You can disconnect sync or delete the gist at any time.

Sign-ins

Some sites (SmartBuy, Amazon) show results only when you are signed in. The extension uses your existing browser session for those sites, the same way any open tab does. It never sees or stores your passwords.

Data sale and use

No data is sold, shared, used for advertising, or used for any purpose other than showing you fare comparisons.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/prashant300/fare-scout.
